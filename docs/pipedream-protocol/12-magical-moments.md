**6. The 30% Waterfall** — L7
Trigger: creator payout flow. See: your revenue river hits the platform dam; exactly 30% pours into a side canyon labeled *distribution* before reaching your tank. Slider lets you try other platforms’ cuts live.

**7. Seed Phrase Constellation** — L8

**8. Consensus Sunrise**
Trigger: block seal. See: validators across the globe ignite in sequence as dawn sweeps the map west→east; lanterns converge glow onto the new block.
Why: consensus feels like a natural phenomenon, not jargon — awe does the teaching.
Hint: time-of-day tint sweep + ordered node glow list from trace event `consensus`. (S)

**9. Mirror Worlds Split-Screen**
